Multiwavelength Classification of J170147.3-421407 Field using MUWCLASS

Project goals

We plan to develop an automated multiwavelength machine-learning classification pipeline (MUWCLASS) to identify the nature of X-ray sources that have been observed by Chandra Source Catalog v2.0.

Work performed

We collected multwavelength properties of all X-ray sources in these fields using the Chandra X-ray Source catalog and multiwavelength properties from Gaia-DR3, 2MASS, and WISE catalogs. We used the machine-learning approach implemented in our MUWCLASS pipeline (see e.g., Yang et al., 2022). The pipeline relies on the training dataset of ~3,000 X-ray sources that have known astrophysical types (currently these are LM-STAR, HM-STAR, YSO, AGN, LMXB, HMXB, CV, NS). We used this pipeline to classify X-ray sources whose classifications are provided in the table below.

Column definitions:
  • name: X-ray name from CSCv2.0, -0 indicates no counterpart, -i indicates different counterparts
  • Class: classification from MUWCLASS
  • Class_prob,Class_prob_e: classification probability and its uncertainty
  • CT: classification confidence threshold
  • CSC_RA,CSC_DEC,CSC_err_r0,CSC_err_r1,CSC_PA: X-ray coordinate and its error ellipse in arcsec
  • significance: X-ray significance
  • Fcsc_{b,s,m,h}: X-ray band fluxes at the broad (0.5-7 keV), soft (0.5-1.2 keV), medium (1.2-2 keV), hard (2-7 keV) bands
  • HR_hms: X-ray hardness ratios
  • var_intra_prob, var_inter_prob: X-ray intra-observation (within one observation) and inter-observation (between observation) variability probability
  • p_any,p_i: NWAY association probability and the probability of each ith counterpart
  • MW_RA,MW_DEC,MW_err0,MW_sep:counterpart coordinate, its positional uncertainty (in arcsec), and separation to the X-ray position
  • Gaia_DR3Name,CATWISE_Name,AllWISE_Name,TMASS_Name
  • Gmag,BPmag,RPmag: Gaia DR3 magnitudes
  • RPlx: Gaia DR3 Parallax divided by its standard error
  • PM: Gaia DR3 Total proper motion, in arcsec/yr
  • rgeo: Gaia EDR3 geometric distance posterior, in pc
  • Jmag,Hmag,Kmag: 2MASS magnitudes
  • W1mag,W2mag,W3mag: WISE magnitudes
  • color:marker color of different classification in the interactive plot
